Help shape future updates by sending gameplay or design feedback through the **Send game feedback** link below the game. Feedback is tracked as a public GitHub issue.

## Feedback operations
- Player feedback: https://github.com/dbpimg/sb/issues/new?template=game-feedback.yml
- Maintainer issue list: https://github.com/dbpimg/sb/issues
- GitHub issue submissions may require players to sign in to GitHub; the game itself does not require sign-in. Do not advertise feedback as anonymous or account-free.
- Review feedback weekly; prioritize crashes, unusable touch targets and broken game flow before new features.
- Avoid collecting player names, email addresses or other personal data in issue templates.
- Do not add third-party analytics until the privacy notice and consent implications are reviewed.

## Launch verification
Confirm the Pages workflow is green for the release commit, then manually test mobile and desktop gameplay. Validate that sharing uses the device's native share sheet or copies the link when available. No third-party SDKs are used for sharing.

## Remaining publicity items
- Export the social-preview SVG into a PNG/JPG for sites that cannot render SVG previews.
- Capture real current screenshots and footage from the running game; do not substitute promotional mockups as gameplay captures.
- Prepare dedicated YouTube Playables SDK integration separately.
