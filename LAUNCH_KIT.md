## Store / listing description
**Jack Of Spades Marketing Games Quick Rummy** brings classic card-table atmosphere to a fast single-player browser game. Draw from the stock or discard pile, organize your cards by suits or matching ranks, build runs and sets, and knock with ten or fewer deadwood points. Play against the computer, get an optional discard suggestion, toggle sounds, and pick up where you left off with local browser saving. Works on desktop and mobile. No sign-up, payment, or download required.

This is an original **seven-card Quick Rummy variant**, not an official Gin Rummy tournament implementation.

## Media inventory
- `assets/game-icon.svg`: original scalable branded icon; suitable favicon and source for raster export.

Some social crawlers require **PNG or JPEG**, not SVG, for link previews. Before paid distribution or a wide launch, export the banner as a 1200 × 630 PNG, commit it, and switch `og:image` and `twitter:image` to the raster asset. Check social previews in major platforms before relying on them.
